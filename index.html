Why there is so much space

These sessions are spacious.

A few words, and then room. Long pauses between the lines, so there is time to arrive, to notice what is here, and to let something settle or open on its own. You are not missing anything in the quiet. The quiet is the practice, and the music is there to hold you in it.

Go at your own pace. Listen once or a hundred times, for the whole session or for a few minutes, eyes closed or open. Nothing here needs to be done right.
