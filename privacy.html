Privacy policy

Superthoughts respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

  • What we collect

    • Name and email address. When you sign up for notifications or purchase a program, we collect the name and email you provide.
    • Payment information. When you purchase a program, payment is processed by Stripe. We do not store your card number. Stripe may collect additional information as described in its privacy policy.
    • Usage data. We collect basic analytics (pages visited, browser type) to understand how people use the site. We do not use invasive tracking or sell data to advertisers.

  • How we use your information

    • To deliver program content you requested or purchased.
    • To send you emails related to programs you signed up for, through Kit, our email service provider.
    • To process your purchase and provide support.
    • To improve the site and our offerings.

    We never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing.

  • Email

    When you sign up, you receive emails related to the programs you expressed interest in. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time and we honor the request promptly.

  • Third-party services

    We use Kit for email delivery and subscriber management, Stripe for payment processing, and GitHub Pages for hosting. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

  • Cookies

    This site uses the minimal cookies needed for functionality and analytics. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

  • Data security

    The whole site is served over HTTPS. Payment processing is handled by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission over the internet is fully secure.

  • Your rights

    You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing josh@superthoughts.com. We respond within a reasonable time.

  • Children's privacy

    This site and our programs are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

  • Changes to this policy

    If we make significant changes to this policy, we update the date below. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
